King's Award for Voluntary Service

21 July

We came together to celebrate Sue's House receiving the King's Award for Voluntary Service. The award was presented by Lieutenant Colonel David Utting and Colonel Jane Davies.

It was an afternoon of celebration, live music and unity. Thank you to every volunteer and patient for your continued support.
