About Aloe Vera

There are over 200 varieties of Aloe with Aloe Barbadensis Miller being the true healing type. It has been used for centuries to maintain health and enhance beauty, both inside and out.

Aloe Vera contains over 75 active nutrients. It encourages good absorption from our food and other supplements and helps to detoxify our bodies.
