Trustees

Suhaila Tauheed

Having a strong medical background and vast experience as a counsellor and psychotherapist, Suhaila became a Trustee of Sue’s House in March 2015. Suhaila helps make sure Sue’s House is carrying out its purposes for which it was set up in 1984. She also assisted Gerald Reading in maintaining the smooth running of all day to day responsibilities. Since the death of Gerald Reading in January 2021, Suhaila has been responsible for making sure Sue’s House is kept up to date with Charity Commission and all other authorities’ requirements, especially during the Covid-19 pandemic, ensuring the charity is adhering to all government guidelines.

Simon Lukes

I was introduced to Sue's House after spending a week at the Bristol Cancer Help Centre (now called Penny Brohn Cancer Care) in 2002. I am a great believer in emotional healing and I have been coming to Sue's House for treatment ever since. I have worked and volunteered in the voluntary/community sector most of my adult life mainly in "hands-on" roles but also doing a lot of administration and management.

Aqida Abbasi

I joined Sue's House this year as a volunteer. This is a wonderful charity, very friendly, enthusiastic and great team to work with. I am a retired NHS nurse practitioner with 46 years of service. I am a community person bringing in experiences of working with other charities, involved with Seven Kings Hubb project as well as working part time with my local school as an exam invigilator. I have served the community as a Justice of the Peace for over 15 years.
