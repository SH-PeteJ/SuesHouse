Our trustees

Our trustees bring experience, compassion and strategic oversight to support the work of Sue's House and help guide the charity in delivering high-quality services and sustainable care.

Suhaila Tauheed

Trustee

Having a strong medical background and vast experience as a counsellor and psychotherapist, Suhaila became a Trustee of Sue's House in March 2015. She helps ensure Sue's House remains true to its original purpose and continues to operate effectively in line with its charitable objectives.

Since the death of Gerald Reading in January 2021, Suhaila has been responsible for maintaining compliance with Charity Commission requirements and other regulatory obligations, particularly during the Covid-19 pandemic.

Simon Lukes

Trustee

I was introduced to Sue's House after spending a week at the Bristol Cancer Help Centre in 2002. I am a strong believer in emotional healing and have been receiving support at Sue's House ever since. I have worked and volunteered in the voluntary and community sector for most of my adult life, with experience across practical, administrative and leadership roles.

Aqida Abbasi

Trustee

I joined Sue's House as a volunteer and have been inspired by the warmth, enthusiasm and commitment of the team. I am a retired NHS nurse practitioner with 46 years of service and bring a strong community-focused perspective shaped by my work with local charities and public service.

My background includes long-standing involvement with the Seven Kings Hub project and work as a Justice of the Peace, alongside experience supporting community wellbeing and education initiatives.
