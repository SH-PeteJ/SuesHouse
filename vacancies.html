Why volunteer with us?

Twenty years ago, around 1 in 10 people would be diagnosed with cancer during their lifetime. Today, it’s 1 in 2.

Behind every diagnosis is a person, a family, a friend, a colleague, a child, a parent. The latest statistics also suggest that around 1 in 4 families will experience cancer affecting a child or baby, highlighting the growing impact cancer has on communities everywhere.

As more people face cancer, more support is needed.

That’s why places like Sue’s House in Ilford matter so much.

Sue’s House provides free counselling, complementary therapies and emotional support for people affected by cancer. Their doors are always open, and they never turn anyone away.

But they are entirely volunteer-run and rely on donations and fundraising to continue their incredible work.
