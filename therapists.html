Counsellor

Suhaila Tauheed

MBACP, FHCP

Suhaila qualified in Counselling and Humanistic Psychology in 1999, covering cognitive, person-centred, humanistic and Gestalt approaches. Her experience includes one-to-one and couples counselling, bereavement support, anxiety, depression, addiction and the effects of abuse.

She joined Sue's House in 2010 after training with Macmillan Cancer Support and developing a deeper understanding of how cancer affects patients and families. She provides empathetic, non-judgemental, safe and confidential counselling to patients and carers.

“I help patients and carers process and express their issues and cope better with life's troubles.”
