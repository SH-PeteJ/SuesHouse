King's Award for Voluntary Service

On 21st July we came together to celebrate Sue’s House’s King's Award for Voluntary Service. The award was presented by Lieutenant Colonel David Utting and Colonel Jane Davies. An afternoon of well-deserved celebration, live music and unity. A huge thank you to all the volunteers and patients for their continued support.
